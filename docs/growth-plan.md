# Chef Healthy Henry: Growth and Ads Plan

Last updated: October 2026. Owner: Josh (growth/tech) + Henry (content/voice).
Written for whoever picks up paid acquisition, podcasts, or creator buys.

This plan was drafted while designing the membership. None of the paid
channels below have been run yet, so every cost figure here is an industry
estimate, not something measured on this account. Treat them as starting
hypotheses to test, not as forecasts.

For the funnel, email rules, and SEO detail, read `docs/marketing-plan.md`
alongside this. Where the two disagree on whether ads and memberships are in
scope, this document is the current one.

## The one number

Email subscribers per week, by source. Every channel below gets a UTM-tagged
link and its own signup source tag in Resend. Cut anything that costs more
than about $3 per subscriber. Scale what beats it.

Rough targets from the launch plan: 1,000 subscribers before the paid push,
5,000 before Chef's Table opens.

## Paid ads

The fastest lever, and the one with real budget risk. Start at $300 to $500
a month.

**Meta only to begin with.** Henry's audience is on Facebook, and the
Protein Flip™ community group is the proof. Skip Google Ads: the recipe SEO
already covers search intent for free, so paid search would be buying
traffic the site can earn.

Two campaigns:

1. **Lead ads for the free guides.** The Grocery Store Test and the GLP-1
   guide, traded for an email. Target healthy-cooking, high-protein, and
   GLP-1 or weight-management adjacent interests. Estimate $1 to $3 per
   email lead, so $300 to $500 a month buys roughly 150 to 400 subscribers.
2. **Retargeting** site visitors and email openers with the cookbook, and
   later the membership. Historically the cheapest conversions available,
   and worth running as soon as there is enough traffic to build an
   audience from.

The discipline that matters: do not scale either campaign until the path
from landing page to email to membership converts. Ads into a leaky funnel
burn money, which is why the July plan deferred them in the first place.

## Podcasts

**Guest appearances first, paid shoutouts second.**

Pitch Henry to 10 to 15 midsize shows, roughly 5k to 50k downloads an
episode, in healthy cooking, GLP-1, and over-50 wellness. The pitch is his
story: trained chef, the Protein Flip™ Method, the cookbook, his own
transformation. Every appearance ends the same way, with the free Grocery
Store Test at chefhealthyhenry.com.

Paid host-read shoutouts on niche nutrition and GLP-1 shows run an estimated
$15 to $30 CPM. Test one or two buys at $200 to $500, and only after free
appearances show the message converts.

## Creators and influencers

The highest-variance lever. Budget it as a series of small tests.

**Paid micro-creators, 10k to 100k followers**, in high-protein cooking,
GLP-1 journey, and over-50 fitness. Pay roughly $100 to $500 a post for a
dedicated Reel or TikTok cooking one of Henry's free recipes and pointing to
the free guide. Buy 3 to 5 tests, give each a tracked link, and double down
only on the ones that move signups.

**Free collaborations cost nothing and may work better.** Invite creators to
Protein Flip their own signature dish, with Henry duetting or reacting.
Content for both sides, and it seeds examples for the Recipe Makeover
feature.

No macro or big-name buys until the funnel is proven. A $5,000 post into a
leaky funnel is the classic way to waste a launch budget.

## SEO, in brief

Covered properly in `docs/marketing-plan.md`. The short version: the 201
recipe pages are the workhorse, the priority queries are "high protein
[dish]" and GLP-1 meal searches, and the single best unbuilt asset is a free
GLP-1 recipes landing page with an email capture, since Henry already sells
a GLP-1 guide and that search category is growing.

## Launch sequencing

Written before the build; most of the build is now done. Kept because the
sequence of the promotional moves still holds.

- **Seed before launch.** Two or three Application Lessons, a Kitchen Guide
  if one is natural, the first Featured Ingredient, and one demonstration
  Recipe Makeover seeded from a family recipe so the feature exists before
  member submissions do.
- **Test as a member before anyone pays.** Sign up, pay, sign in, find the
  lesson, join the group, cancel, rejoin, all on a phone.
- **Henry reviews Sous before launch.** He approves roughly the first 20
  answers, since it speaks in his name.
- **Launch as a founding-member moment.** First email to the list: join in
  week one and lock the $120 annual price for life. Real scarcity, honestly
  framed.
- **Chef's Table at about month 6**, offered to existing members first at
  $49 a month locked for life against a $69 public price, sold against the
  $95 single coaching session.

## What we measure

Three numbers, checked weekly: email subscribers per week by source,
free-to-paid conversion, and monthly member churn. Everything else is noise
until those are healthy.
