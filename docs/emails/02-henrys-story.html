The story behind the Protein Flip™

I trained as a chef in Chicago about twenty years ago. Technique was never my problem. The weight crept on anyway, and what unsettled me more than the scale was feeling disconnected in the one place that had always anchored me: the kitchen.

I tried the diets. Subtraction, restriction, willpower. They all worked for a month and cost me the joy of cooking, which meant none of them lasted.

What finally worked was addition. I kept the meat, the carbs, and the bold spices, and I rebuilt the proportions of my plate so protein leads and vegetables do more of the lifting. I stayed full. My blood sugar steadied. And cooking became joyful again, which is the real reason it stuck.

That is the whole method. No counting, no forbidden foods, just a different way of building the plate you already love. If you want to see what it looks like in practice, my story and the before-and-after are on the site.
