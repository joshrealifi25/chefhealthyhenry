# Application Lesson Ideas

- **The Acid Finish** — dish tastes flat, seasoning isn't fixing it; covers lemon/lime/vinegar/yogurt, when to add, how Protein Flip™ builds it in
- **What Umami Actually Does** — dish tastes fine but not satisfying; umami is depth not flavor; parmesan rind, miso, fish sauce, tomato paste, mushrooms, soy — when to reach for them ← *this month*
- **Building a Plate When Appetite Is Unpredictable** — three-part small plate structure; soft anchor + cold bite + warm bite; ties into WNSG guide
- **Cooking in Order: What Goes in the Pan First and Why** — heat + fat + order; why aromatics bloom or burn, proteins sear or steam, vegetables caramelize or go limp
